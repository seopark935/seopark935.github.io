Hello, and welcome to my project portfolio! My name is Seohyun Park, and I am a Computer Engineering major at Georgia Tech (Graduating May 2027) with threads in [Computer Hardware & Emerging Architectures](https://ece.gatech.edu/computing-hardware-emerging-architectures-thread) and [Systems & Architecture](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/academics/threads/systems-architecture), interested primarily in physical design and computer architecture, with some side work in bioinformatics and high-thoroughput data analysis.

I was a physical design intern in Apple's SoC Silicon Engineering Group (Summer 2027), as well as a computational drug discovery researcher (and co-lead author, see Publications) in the [Liang Lab](https://med.emory.edu/departments/biochemistry/research-labs/liang/index.html) at Emory University. Additionally, I worked as a physical design verification lead within in Georgia Tech's [SiliconJackets club](https://siliconjackets.gt/).

This site highlights key projects and accomplishments, demonstrating my technical skills in various areas of interest.
